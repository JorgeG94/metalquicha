   !! Lebedev-Laikov octahedral orbit parameters, orders 6 to 5810
   !!
   !! Each record is one orbit of the octahedral group: a code saying which
   !! orbit type, the seed parameters a and b, and the weight carried by every
   !! point in it. `mqc_lebedev` expands them into points. Records for order
   !! LEBEDEV_ORDERS(i) run from ORDER_OFFSET(i) to ORDER_OFFSET(i+1)-1.
   !!
   !! These are the published Lebedev-Laikov parameters [V.I. Lebedev and
   !! D.N. Laikov, Dokl. Math. 59, 477 (1999)] and integrate polynomials up to
   !! degree 131 to a relative accuracy of 2e-14. The weights of each grid sum
   !! to 1, so the surface of the unit sphere is normalised out.
   !!
