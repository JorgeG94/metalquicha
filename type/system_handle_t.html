system_handle_t

type, public :: system_handle_t

The system, plus what the caller has actually told us about it

bonds_declared is not the same as having bonds. A caller that has said “no bonds” and a caller that has not got round to it look identical in system_geometry_t, and one of them is about to fragment a covalent molecule into radicals.
