severed_bond_t

type, public :: severed_bond_t

One bond that a partition cuts, and enough about it to decide what to do Atom indices are 1-based here, unlike bond_t, because the callers that want this – fragment assembly and the frozen-orbital schemes – index the system directly rather than through a deck.
