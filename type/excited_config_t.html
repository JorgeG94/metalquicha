excited_config_t

type, public :: excited_config_t

Linear-response excited states: how many, of what kind, how they solve

Backend-neutral in shape, and separate from cc_config_t on purpose: these are the TDHF/TDDFT roots of the reference itself, not an EOM treatment on top of a coupled-cluster wave function. The cenzontle backend solves it, on an Energy driver; see excited_decline_reason in mqc_czt_bridge for the calculations it is refused on and why.
