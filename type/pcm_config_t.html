pcm_config_t

type, public :: pcm_config_t

A polarizable continuum: the cavity, the solvent, and the charge solve

Backend-neutral in shape, but only the cuEST path implements it. tblite’s CPCM is configured through xtb_config_t and builds its own cavity; the two are separate models and share no settings.
