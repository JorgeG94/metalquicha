# Nuclear basis sets for quantum protons

Generated by `tools/neo/convert_nuclear_basis.py` from `pyscf/neo/basis/*.dat`
in Yang Yang's PySCF fork (https://github.com/theorychemyang/pyscf, commit
f9c0266 of 2026-08-25). The PB4-D through PB6-H sets are the even-tempered
proton bases of Yu, Pavosevic and Hammes-Schiffer, J. Chem. Phys. 152,
244123 (2020); `dzsnb` is the older GAMESS NEO set; `1s1p`, `2s1p` and `1e6`
are the fork's small test sets. Element 1 only, one primitive per shell.

These are tracked files, unlike the Basis Set Exchange bundle unpacked into
`basis_sets/`, and `basis_search_path` looks in this directory after it.
