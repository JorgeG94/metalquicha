# Pople sets the Basis Set Exchange does not carry

`6-311++g(3df,2p).json` is generated by `tools/basis/make_6-311ppg_3df_2p.py`
from the BSE `6-311++g(3df,3pd)` entry in the bundle: heavy atoms unchanged,
hydrogen with the split-polarization p pair 1.5 and 0.375 and no d. It is the
basis GAMESS recommends for MAKEFP and the one the EFMO paper (Sattasathuchana
et al., JCTC 20, 2445, 2024) uses. Checked shell for shell against PySCF's own
table for H, C, N, O, and the water RHF energy agrees to 1e-10.

Tracked files; `basis_search_path` looks in this directory after `basis_sets/`.
