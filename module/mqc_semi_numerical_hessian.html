Builds a Hessian from central differences of a method’s analytic gradients:

H[i,j] = (g_j(x_i + h) - g_j(x_i - h)) / 2h

Semi-numerical: only one derivative is taken numerically, the other is analytic. Cost is 6N gradient evaluations for N atoms, plus the undisplaced point, each of them a full SCF. Hessian from central differences of gradients
