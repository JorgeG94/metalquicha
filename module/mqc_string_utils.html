int_to_text

Formatting helpers for building messages and JSON paths.

pic_io’s to_char already does this and is what most of the program uses, but it is not pure, so it cannot be called from the validators and readers that are. That is the whole reason six near-identical private copies of “integer to string” had accumulated – as int_to_string, int_text and integer_to_key – rather than anyone preferring their own.

So: use to_char in ordinary code, and this where pure is required.
