Defines integer constants for quantum chemistry methods to avoid string comparisons throughout the codebase. Provides conversion utilities between string representations and integer constants. Input-file spelling -> method type Spin-component scaling a spelling implies Whether a spelling asks for RI

Effective fragment potentials (60-69). Unlike every other entry here, this solves no wavefunction of its own: each fragment carries one already, computed when its potential was made, and what is evaluated is the interaction between them.

Interaction energies that come out decomposed (60-69). Unlike the entries above, these do not return the energy of a system: they return the interaction between two of them, broken into named physical terms.

60 is EFP2 on feat/efp-efp; SAPT0 takes 61 so the two branches can merge without either constant moving.
