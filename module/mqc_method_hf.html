Hartree-Fock, and the MP2 and coupled-cluster corrections built on it.

Dispatches to whichever backend model.backend names: the CPU one through mqc_czt_bridge, or the GPU one through mqc_cuest_bridge. cuEST has no conventional four-index ERI path, so J and K are always density-fitted there and an auxiliary (JKFIT) basis is required alongside the orbital basis.
