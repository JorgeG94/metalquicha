Closed-shell Hartree-Fock.

With the cuEST backend (CMake, MQC_ENABLE_CUEST=ON) the integrals come the GPU and the SCF runs in mqc_cuest_scf. Because cuEST offers no conventional four-index ERI path, J and K are always density-fitted, so an auxiliary (JKFIT) basis is required alongside the orbital basis.

Without that backend the method reports a clear error rather than a placeholder number – a silently wrong energy inside a many-body expansion is far worse than a failed fragment.
